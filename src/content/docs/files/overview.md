Find templates, 3D models, and other resources for your projects!

## Open Source Resources

### [Protogen 3D Prints](/resources/3d-prints/)
Free STL files for various Protogen head designs including Sigma, Epsilon, Delta, Beta, and Gamma variants.

### [Protogen Electronics](/resources/electronics/)
PCB designs, schematics, and Gerber files for Protocontroller V1, V2, and SmartMatrix Shield.

### [ProtoTracer Helpers](/resources/prototracer-helpers/)
Python scripts for converting 3D models, images, and GIFs into ProtoTracer-compatible formats.
