This guide covers everything you need to know to set up and use your KB WS35 Protogen Kit, including wiring, LED panels, the Protocontroller V1.1, sensors, and general recommendations.

## Guide Sections

- [**Wiring Your Protogen**](./wiring/) - Sensor wiring diagram and wiring pictures
- [**KB WS35 LED Panels**](./led-panels/) - LED panel information, pin assignments, and diagrams
- [**Protocontroller V1.1**](./protocontroller-v1/) - Pinout, schematic, physical design, and header pinouts
- [**Sensors and Peripherals**](./sensors/) - Boop sensor, microphone, control button, programming links, and recommendations

## Related Resources

:::note[Open Source Files]
The PCB designs, schematics, and fabrication files for this kit are available in the [Protogen Electronics](/resources/electronics/) repository. You can fabricate your own boards or modify the design to suit your needs.
:::

For complete programming instructions, refer to the [ProtoTracer Documentation](https://coelacant.com/ProtoTracer/). You can also use the [ProtoTracer Helper Tools](/resources/prototracer-helpers/) to convert custom 3D models and images.
